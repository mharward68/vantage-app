## Then the review pass — the light-mode seam, and it was two faults not one

Michael saw a faint vertical join where the sidebar meets the header, in light theme only. Both causes were measured before anything was edited, and neither was the tint.

- **Cause 1, the ground. `#canvas-header` sat on `rgba(244,245,247,.25)` over `#main-canvas`'s `--color-base` `#f4f5f7` — which composites to `#f4f5f7` EXACTLY**, because blending a colour with itself changes nothing, so that alpha had never bought any translucency. `#sidebar` sat on `--color-sidebar-bg` `#eaecef`. **A 10/9/8 RGB step, straight down the seam.**
- **Why twelve screenshots missed it:** in dark the same two tokens are `#09090b` and `#0c0c0e`, a step of 3 — invisible. A defect that can only exist in one theme gets out-voted by the theme that hides it.
- **Fix: `#canvas-header`'s ground is now `var(--color-sidebar-bg)`.** Both halves of the strip sit on one token in both themes, so the step is zero by construction. The `.light-theme #canvas-header` override went with it (the token already differs per theme), and so did `backdrop-filter` — an opaque background makes a backdrop blur inert, and `#canvas-body` is the header's flex **sibling**, not content passing beneath it. `--backdrop-blur` has seven other live users and stays.
- **Cause 2, the ramps. The sidebar's tint runs vertically over the full 874px column; the header's runs horizontally, so along the seam it holds `--hub-tint-strong` for the strip's whole 72px.** A two-stop vertical ramp had already decayed to **27.5% against the header's 30%** by the bottom of the strip — a 1/5/2 wedge on top of the base step.
- **Fix: the sidebar gradient is now THREE stops**, holding the strong stop to `var(--app-strip-height)` before it fades. The two gradients are identical down the entire seam, and the decay becomes what it should be — a property of the column below the band, not of the band.
- **Verified after the fix:** `groundsMatch: true`, both `rgb(234,236,239)` in light and `rgb(12,12,14)` in dark; sidebar ramp resolves to `…/0.3) 0px, …/0.3) 72px, …/0) 100%`; `headerBackdrop: none`. Screenshots re-taken in light CampaignHub (the reported case), dark Dashboard, and collapsed light DataHub.
- **One scare worth not repeating:** the first measurement after each reload reported BOTH grounds as `rgb(12,12,14)` — the dark value — while `body.className` said `light-theme`. `document.getAnimations().length` was **108**. The document parses with no theme class, `initTheme()` adds it, the `background-color` transition starts, and a hidden tab never advances it, so computed style reports the START value forever. **The transition kill-switch must be re-injected after EVERY reload, not once per session.** Filed.
