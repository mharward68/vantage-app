## Assumptions made

- **`newHistoryId(prospect)` guarantees uniqueness; C5's entry shape does not change.** C5 quotes `hist-${Date.now()}` from `recordInteraction()`, which collides inside a bulk loop — several tasks complete in the same millisecond. That is not cosmetic: `deleteInteraction()` filters on `h.id !== histId`, so deleting one entry would silently delete every twin. The helper appends `-1`, `-2` only on collision, following the plan's own `task-${Date.now()}-${i}` restore precedent (Assumption 8). Verified: three bulk completions produced `hist-…529`, `-1`, `-2`, three unique of three. **Michael reviewed this and accepted it, 2026-08-29** (*"I will just trust your judgement"*). Settled: a uniqueness guarantee inside the frozen shape, not a contract change. Use `newHistoryId()` for any future history writer.
- **A bulk action clears the selection; it does not clear the sticky set.** Re-running Mark Complete on rows just completed is never the intent, and the AQ precedent removes acted-on records from `aqSelectedIds`. The rows stay visible via `taskStickyCompletedIds` until the filter changes. Reversible: one line.
- **An orphan task completes, logs nothing, and the alert says so by count.** There is no prospect to write history to. Refusing to complete it would strand it; completing it silently would be the invisible-failure mode this session exists to prevent. The alert points at the Missing Prospect chip. Reversible.
