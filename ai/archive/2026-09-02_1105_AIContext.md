## What was done

**Finding 1 — the geography matcher.** One `US_STATES` map (50 + DC, `usStatesCount: 51` verified live) plus a derived reverse lookup, and **one comparison function that both directory branches call.** The shipped code carried two separate near-copies; the plan warned that fixing one is the obvious half-fix, so instead of fixing both they were replaced by `geoFormsMatchRecord(forms, rec)` and the company branch's fan-out over its contacts now goes through the same function.

Three rules, each load-bearing:

1. **The state field compares as a WHOLE VALUE, never a substring.** That single change is what kills the West Virginia bleed, and it is only safe because `state` is a controlled one-of-51 field.
2. **City and metro stay substring matches**, as the plan directed — they are genuinely partial-text ("Northern Virginia", "DC Metro"). **The accepted consequence, stated plainly: a METRO field reading "West Virginia" is still returned by a search for `Virginia`.** The state field is the one that had to be exact and now is.
3. **A two-letter form matches city/metro only as a WHOLE WORD** (split on commas *and* whitespace). This is the shipped `term.length === 2` caution kept intact — "va" is inside Ne**va**da, Syl**va**nia, **Va**ncouver. **The lookup changed, not the caution.**

Because every term expands to both forms *before* anything is compared, **`VA` and `Virginia` are the same query by construction**, not two branches that happen to agree.

**Finding 4 — the widths.** One rule, `#view-prospects .prospects-directory-card .card-header-actions .search-box-wrapper { flex: 1; max-width: 400px; margin: 0; }`, and **Row 1's inline `style` deleted** so all three come from one place. Scoped to this card on purpose: `.search-box-wrapper` also appears in MediaHub and twice in CampaignHub, each still carrying its own inline copy, and a bare `.search-box-wrapper` rule would have silently taken over three surfaces this session did not look at.

**The placeholder** now says what the field supports. It was the half of Finding 1 that made the filter read as *broken* rather than *limited* — it instructed the user to type the one thing that could never match. Measured at 310.2px against 365px of available width: no clipping.
