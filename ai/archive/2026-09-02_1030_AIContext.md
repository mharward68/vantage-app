## Assumptions logged this session

1. **INCLUDE-ONLY ON THIS HUB, AND IT IS ONE WORD TO REVERSE.** The downstream filter classifies each term into company-tag and prospect-tag queries and matches by substring; **it has no NOT semantics anywhere**, and its both-lists OR branch is the most tangled logic in the view. Offering "− Exclude" before the filter can honour it is the control lying about what the results mean (Ladder 2). Delete the `includeOnly: true` line and exclusion appears the moment the filter grows a matching branch. **This is the thing on your list — see "Needs my eyes."**
2. **The `onChange` refocus lives on the hub config, NOT inside `setAqPickerSelection()`.** Selecting an option rebuilds the dropdown, destroying the button just activated and dropping focus to `<body>`; without the refocus, a second tag is a walk from the top of the document. Putting it in the shared function would have changed focus behaviour inside the Advanced Query modal, which is deferred.
3. **`#prospect-tag-chooser` is now the id of the WRAPPER `<div>`.** P8's "the id is the contract, the widget is not." Anything that located this filter by id still finds it; `check_ids.py` sees no id removed.
4. **The three verification interventions were harness-only and touched no file:** the transition kill-switch (standing practice, removed at the end), `snapshotRedModalShownThisSession = true` to stop a blocking `alert()`, and `dd.classList.add("hidden")` to un-cover the chips for a screenshot.
5. **The picker's selection is module state and is deliberately NOT persisted** — same lifetime the `<select>`'s DOM selection had (survives view switches, gone on reload). P9: no new persisted field.
