## Assumptions made

- **Day and week buckets are the most recent buckets that CONTAIN snapshots, not the last 14 / 8 calendar periods counted back from today.** Read as calendar windows, a fortnight away from the app would leave rules 2 and 3 matching nothing and collapse retention to the rolling 10 on the very first run back — pruning history at the moment it is least replaceable. The bucket reading can only ever keep more.
- **Mirror cadence is tracked in a module-scope variable, not in state.** It describes this machine's filesystem, like `snapshotHealth`, and unlike `snapshotHealth` it needs no storage at all: the mirror is idempotent, so a forgotten run costs one directory listing and writes nothing. This keeps `state.snapshotHealth`'s C13 shape untouched and adds nothing to backups that would need stripping.
- **The mirror never deletes**, including when an attachment is deleted in the app — an older snapshot may still reference it.
- Verification ran against **seed data** in the extension-driven Chrome profile, as in 1.1 — *not* Michael's production database.
