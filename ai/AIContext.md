## What was done

Four of the six hubs are now on the S5 idiom. The header band (2A.4) is unblocked once 2A.3 lands.

- **ProspectHub was NOT already converted**, contrary to the note 2A.1 left. It fit one screen only because its two directory tables carried an inline `max-height: 35vh` each, which happens to total less than a screen — `#view-prospects` had no height at all, so `.prospects-layout-container`'s `height: 100%` was resolving against an auto-height parent, i.e. to `auto`. - **The two `35vh` constants are gone.** The two directory tables stay two tables with two scrollbars (scope §2.4) and now flex against the panel instead of the window — `.hub-scroll` on each `.table-scroll-container`.
- **Michael's review changed the split, twice, and both changes shipped in this session.** The first pass gave the two sections an equal share (`flex: 1 1 0` each). He asked instead for **Contacts to expand and Companies to contract with its results, but never to grow taller than that even split.** So Contacts is `flex: 1 1 auto` and Companies is `flex: 0 0 auto; max-height: calc(50% - 8px)` — content-sized, hard-capped. `0 0` rather than `0 1` is deliberate: with shrink enabled the two flex in proportion to their content heights and the split drifts with the row counts instead of the cap deciding it. The `- 8px` is the wrapper's 16px gap, which belongs to neither child, so the cap lands exactly on the 366px the even split produced.
- **And then: one directory at a time.** "See All Contacts" hides the Companies section outright and gives Contacts the whole panel; "See All Companies" does the reverse. This is the session's **only `app.js` edit** — a six-line class toggle in `renderProspectsView()` reading the `state.forceShowAll*` flags that already drove those two buttons. It writes nothing, adds no state field and no backup surface. Both buttons live in the always-visible header bar, so a hidden section is always one click from coming back, and any search or Clear Filters clears the flags on its own.
- **Measured live through the real listeners**, not by setting flags: default `only-contacts` → Contacts 748px / Companies `display: none`; See All Companies → Companies 748px / Contacts `none`; Clear Filters → Contacts 592 / Companies 140 (its content); search `"e"` (4 contacts, 2 companies) → Contacts 532 / Companies 200. `#canvas-body` never scrolls in any of them.
